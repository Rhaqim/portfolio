An agency site has to sell a way of thinking, not just a portfolio. Ludine
studies businesses, people and culture, then turns what it finds into strategy,
campaigns and creative direction. The site needed to make that process legible
to a prospective client in a single scroll.

## Structure

The page walks from approach to proof: the three lenses the agency works
through (business, people, context), a six-step process from research to
impact, four capability areas, and the Brand Room, a diagnostic offer that ends
in a brand opportunity map. Work and writing live under Thinking, with room to
grow as case studies are published.

## Build

Astro, shipping static HTML with interactivity only where the content expands
on demand. The site stays fast on mobile connections and has no client-side
runtime to maintain between content updates.
